The Story

Built from
a single chair.

2019: homeless. August 2022: first cut on his brother, in an apartment in Grand Terrace. Then a home shop, a night-market stall — and now, Catch A Fade Chris.

The promise is consistency, quality, and dependability — on every cut. And it's not just fades: trims, sections, layers, texture, and full transformations, built on real knowledge of headshapes, sectioning, and overdirection. Not just chopping away.
